The problem

You're busy running the business. That's exactly when customers reach out.

Calls go unanswered

The phone rings during the lunch rush, mid-haircut, or while you're unloading a delivery. Most callers don't leave a voicemail. They call the next place on the list.

Customers can't find you online

No website, an old menu, or wrong hours on Google. People decide where to go in a few seconds of searching, and if your information isn't there, they pick someone else.

Stock runs out (or piles up)

Reordering from memory means best-sellers sell out and slow items expire on the shelf. Both cost you money every week.

Questions

Good to know.

Will customers know they're talking to an AI?

Yes. The receptionist introduces itself as your business's virtual assistant. Customers get quick answers, and anyone who wants a person can be passed to you or your staff.

What happens when the receptionist can't answer something?

It takes a message or transfers the call to a number you choose, depending on how you'd like it handled. You decide the rules before it goes live.

Do I need to learn new software?

No. We set everything up and run it for you. For inventory, we work with tools like Square, Shopify or Zoho, so if you already use one of them, we build on what you have.

What does the setup fee cover?

Building everything for your business: designing and writing your website, setting up your Google profile, training the receptionist on your menu, services and hours, and loading your inventory. It's paid once.

Can I start small?

Yes. You can start with Starter or Growth and add more later. We'll recommend the smallest package that solves your biggest problem.
